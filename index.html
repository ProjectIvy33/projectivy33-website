01 / The Studio

Independent by design.
Intentional by nature.

Project Ivy 33 is an independent game development studio dedicated to immersive storytelling, memorable characters, and carefully constructed worlds.

Our work brings together dark-fantasy narrative design, meaningful player choices, and a commitment to preserving the creative identity of every project.
