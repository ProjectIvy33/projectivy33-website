Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Project Ivy 33 operates this informational website and the Studio Sync internal archival integration. For privacy inquiries, contact privacy@projectivy33.com.

Website information

This static website does not currently provide visitor accounts, contact forms, analytics scripts, or advertising trackers. Its hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, and request logs in providing the service. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond.

Studio Sync and Google data

Studio Sync is a studio-controlled workflow that uses Google OAuth to access an authorized Google Docs archive document and synchronize a reference index of approved internal directives. It accesses the connected account's Google Docs content as needed to complete that task. It is not a public service, does not sell Google user data, and does not use Google Workspace data to train AI models.

Google OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored as protected GitHub Actions repository secrets, not published in this website's source files. Authorized archive records are retained under the studio's control until deleted or replaced as part of legitimate operations. GitHub and Google may independently process operational data under their own policies.

Sharing, security, and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with hosting and infrastructure providers only to provide the website and authorized integration, or when legally required. We take reasonable measures to limit access. No technical system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may revoke Google account authorization through your Google Account's third-party connections controls. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete existing studio archive records. To ask about stored information or request deletion where applicable, email privacy@projectivy33.com.

Updates

This policy may change when our services or data practices change; material revisions will be reflected on this page with a new date.
